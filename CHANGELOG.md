# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-20

Initial public packaging by **Marcin Kowalik <mkowalik@agh.edu.pl>**.

- WeeWX 5.x skin reproducing the classic wFrog visual language.
- European weather units: °C, hPa, km/h, mm, mm/h, W/m².
- Period views: 3 Hours, 24 Hours, 7 Days, 30 Days, 365 Days.
- Classic wFrog-style `numbers` summary view.
- Temperature/dew-point min/avg/max semantics.
- Wind/gust envelope, direction labels, wind radar, and Beaufort current-wind widget.
- Separate cumulative Rain and Rain Rate charts.
- Optional UV and solar-radiation behavior mapped to the historical wFrog configuration.
- Optional ENVMON extension for Radioactive@Home and QCN data.
- Debian/systemd installer and timer.
